1. Select Article Type

Please select the category that best describes your manuscript. Required files and peer review criteria are customized according to your article type.

Original Research Article

Full-length empirical investigations on pathogen genomics, antimicrobial resistance epidemiology, wastewater metagenomic surveillance, and resistance biology.

5,000–8,000 words Double-Blind Review Raw Data Deposition Required

Review Article

Comprehensive, authoritative critical evaluations of key topics, emerging AMR mechanisms, global surveillance frameworks, or sequencing methodologies.

6,000–10,000 words Peer Reviewed Extensive Bibliography

Short Communication / Rapid Report

Concise, urgent novel findings, outbreak alerts, first reports of high-threat resistance genes (e.g. novel carbapenemases or colistin resistance).

2,500 words max Fast-Track Triage (14 days) Max 3 Figures/Tables

Metagenomic Dataset & Resource Note

Descriptions of open-access genomic datasets, wastewater baseline profiles, curated bioinformatics pipelines, or antimicrobial resistance databases.

3,000 words max FAIR Data Audit Public Accession Mandatory

Method & Bioinformatics Protocol

Detailed, reproducible protocols for sample preparation, targeted enrichment, sequencing workflows, or benchmarking computational tools in metagenomics.

4,000 words max Code Repository Required

Perspective & Policy Commentary

Thoughtful perspectives on One Health surveillance policy, ethical genomics, stewardship interventions, or global health governance.

2,000 words max Editorial Review
