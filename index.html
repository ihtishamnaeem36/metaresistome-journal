Advancing Pathogen Intelligence Through Open Genomic Science

The Journal of MetaResistome (JMR) is an independent, peer-reviewed hybrid journal dedicated to publishing foundational and translational research on antimicrobial resistance (AMR), whole-genome sequencing of high-threat pathogens, wastewater metagenomics, and One Health surveillance.

Governed by active domain researchers and free from commercial publisher constraints, JMR provides rapid, constructive peer review focused on methodological transparency and data reproducibility.
